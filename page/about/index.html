ABOUT

Built for the curious.

StudyWithSTEM is a one-person studio turning dense science into premium cheat sheets, poster-deep infographics and free interactive tools — engineered to be studied, not skimmed. One human, one inbox, and a new drop every Friday.

ONE STUDIO · ONE HUMAN · EVERY FRIDAY
01

One studio. One human. Zero filler.

It started with a night-before-the-exam search for one clean page that didn't exist — and a stubborn refusal to accept that.

Textbook margins are dense. Pretty study guides are shallow. Screenshots of screenshots help no one. So StudyWithSTEM became the rule and the exception at once: every topic deserves one page, engineered like a poster and fact-checked like a paper. If a line earns its place, it stays. If it doesn't, it gets cut — then whatever survives gets cut again.

Today that obsession ships as cheat sheets in PNG and PDF, infographics across every science topic from kinematics to ecology, and a growing toolbox of interactive utilities. Everything is designed, written, checked and maintained by one person — who also reads every message, fixes every erratum, and still gets genuinely excited about a well-set equation.

If you've ever rewritten your notes just to make them prettier — you're exactly where you belong.

04

How every sheet is made.

  1. STEP 01

    Research first

    Every constant, formula and claim is checked against real sources before design touches anything. When two sources disagree, the sheet says so.

  2. STEP 02

    Compress twice

    A sheet earns every line. If it doesn't help you understand — or pass — it gets cut. Then whatever survives gets cut again.

  3. STEP 03

    Design is memory work

    Hierarchy, spacing and color aren't decoration. They decide what your eyes find first — which decides what your brain keeps.

  4. STEP 04

    Errata ship

    Found a mistake? It gets fixed, logged, and the corrected file reaches everyone who already owns the sheet. Corrections are always welcome.
