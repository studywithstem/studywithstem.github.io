03 · THE FORMULAS

Three ways to find a.

Every mode in the calculator is one of these — pick the route that matches what you know.

From velocity & time

a = (vf − vi) ÷ t

The definition of acceleration: how much velocity changes each second. A car going 0 → 27.78 m/s in 8 s gains 3.47 m/s² — about 0.35 g.

From distance & time

a = 2(d − vi·t) ÷ t²

Know the ground covered instead of the final speed? From rest this collapses to a = 2d ÷ t² — 400 m in 20 s means 2 m/s².

From force & mass

a = F ÷ m

Newton's second law. The same 3000 N shove moves a 1200 kg car at 2.5 m/s² but a 30 kg bike at 100 m/s² — mass is the price of inertia.

04 · FAQ

Acceleration, answered.

The questions people actually type into Google — answered plainly, with the numbers to back them up.

What is acceleration?+

Acceleration is the rate at which velocity changes over time — speeding up, slowing down or turning all count. It is measured in metres per second squared (m/s²): a car accelerating at 3 m/s² gains 3 m/s of speed every second.

How do I calculate acceleration?+

Use a = (v_f − v_i) ÷ t — subtract the starting velocity from the final velocity, then divide by the time taken. A car going from 0 to 100 km/h (27.78 m/s) in 8 s accelerates at 27.78 ÷ 8 ≈ 3.47 m/s². Mode 01 above also shows distance covered, average speed and g-force.

How do I find acceleration from distance and time?+

With constant acceleration, a = 2(d − v_i·t) ÷ t². From rest it simplifies to a = 2d ÷ t² — a sprinter covering 100 m in 9.58 s from a standing start averages about 2.18 m/s². Mode 02 does this and also returns the final velocity.

How do I calculate acceleration from force and mass?+

Newton's second law: a = F ÷ m. A net force of 3000 N on a 1200 kg car gives 3000 ÷ 1200 = 2.5 m/s². Keep the units consistent — mode 03 accepts N, kN, lbf and kgf with kg, g, lb or tonnes and converts automatically.

What units is acceleration measured in?+

The SI unit is m/s². You will also meet ft/s² (1 ft/s² = 0.3048 m/s²), km/h per second (1 km/h·s ≈ 0.2778 m/s²), mph per second, and g-force where 1 g = 9.80665 m/s². Mode 04 converts between all of them instantly.

What does 1 g of acceleration mean?+

1 g = 9.80665 m/s² — the acceleration of a freely falling object near Earth's surface, and the pull that gives you your normal weight. For comparison: a hard-braking car reaches about 0.8–1 g, astronauts at launch about 3 g, and fighter jets up to 9 g.

Can acceleration be negative?+

Yes. Negative acceleration means velocity decreases in your chosen positive direction — deceleration, like braking from 100 km/h to 0 in 5 s (−5.56 m/s²). In mode 02 a negative final velocity simply means the object reversed direction during the interval.

It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded, logged, or sent to a server. No account, no sign-up, no limits.
