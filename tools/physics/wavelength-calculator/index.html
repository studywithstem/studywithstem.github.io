03 · THE FORMULAS

Every formula it uses.

Nothing hidden — these six relations power every number on this page.

The wavelength formula

λ = v ÷ f

Wave speed divided by frequency — the distance one full cycle covers. 343 m/s at 440 Hz gives the 0.78 m concert A.

Frequency

f = v ÷ λ

The same triangle rearranged: how many cycles pass a point each second when you already know the spacing and the speed.

Period & wavenumber

T = 1/f · k = 2π/λ

Period is seconds per cycle; wavenumber is radians per metre — the wave’s frequency written in space instead of time.

Photon energy

E = h·c ÷ λ

h = 6.626×10−34 J·s and c = 2.998×108 m/s. Visible light spans about 1.6–3.3 eV, and λ(nm) × E(eV) ≈ 1240.

Speed of sound in air

v = 331.3 × √(1 + T/273.15)

T is the Celsius temperature — at 20 °C this gives 343.2 m/s. The shortcut v ≈ 331.3 + 0.606·T stays close near room temperature.

Wave on a string

v = √(F ÷ µ)

Tension F over linear density µ, square-rooted. Combine with λ = v ÷ f to find the wavelength of the note the string sounds.

What is the formula for wavelength?+

Divide the wave’s speed by its frequency: λ = v ÷ f. A 440 Hz sound wave moving at 343 m/s has a wavelength of 343 ÷ 440 ≈ 0.78 m.

Keep speed in m/s and frequency in Hz and the wavelength comes out in metres — the same triangle also gives f = v ÷ λ and v = λ × f.

How do I calculate the wavelength of a sound wave?+

Take the speed of sound in the medium and divide by the frequency. In air the speed depends on temperature — about 331 m/s at 0 °C and 343 m/s at 20 °C — so a 440 Hz concert A stretches about 0.78 m from compression to compression.

The Sound waves tab computes the speed from your air temperature, or uses preset speeds for water, glass, steel and aluminium.

How do I calculate the wavelength of light?+

Use the speed of light, c = 299,792,458 m/s. Green 532 nm laser light has a frequency of f = c ÷ λ ≈ 563 THz, and each of its photons carries E = hc ÷ λ ≈ 2.33 eV.

The Light & photons tab converts between wavelength, frequency and photon energy in a single step, and names the EM band the value falls in.

Does wavelength change when a wave enters a new medium?+

Yes — but frequency never changes. When light passes from air into water it slows to c ÷ 1.33, so its wavelength shrinks by the same factor: 532 nm green light becomes about 399 nm inside water.

The colour is unchanged, because colour tracks frequency, not wavelength. Pick a medium in the Light & photons tab to see both wavelengths side by side.

What is the wavelength range of visible light?+

Roughly 380 nm (violet) to 750 nm (red). Shorter than 380 nm you are in ultraviolet, then X-rays and gamma rays; longer than 750 nm lies infrared, then microwaves and radio waves.

Visible light covers less than an octave of frequency — a narrow window of the full electromagnetic spectrum.

How are wavelength, frequency and period related?+

Frequency counts cycles per second; period is its flip side in seconds per cycle: T = 1 ÷ f. Wavelength connects them to speed through v = λ × f.

A 100 MHz FM radio wave, for instance, repeats every 10 ns and measures about 3 m from crest to crest.

How do I convert wavelength to photon energy?+

Use E = h × c ÷ λ, where h = 6.626×10−34 J·s is Planck’s constant. Divide the energy in joules by 1.602×10−19 to get electronvolts.

A shortcut worth memorising: λ(nm) × E(eV) ≈ 1240 — handy for quick estimates in physics and chemistry exams.
