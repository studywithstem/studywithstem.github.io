03 · THE FORMULAS

Six lines that power the panel.

Every mode on this page runs on one of these — worth keeping them in arm's reach.

From period

f = 1 / T

The definition. Frequency is just the reciprocal of the period — a cycle lasting 2 ms repeats 500 times each second.

From a wave

f = v / λ

Divide the wave's speed by its wavelength — sound at 343 m/s over a 0.78 m wave is the 440 Hz A4 note.

From rotation

f = ω / 2π

One full turn is 2π radians, so dividing angular speed by 2π gives cycles per second: 377 rad/s → 60 Hz.

Photon energy

E = hf

Planck's constant ties a photon's energy to its frequency — a 2 eV red photon oscillates about 484 trillion times per second.

Light's wavelength

λ = c / f

In a vacuum, wavelength times frequency is always c — 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi waves are 12.5 cm long.

Back to period

T = 1 / f

The reverse move. A 50 Hz mains cycle lasts 20 ms; a 100 MHz FM wave repeats every 10 ns.

04 · QUESTIONS

Frequency, answered.

The things students ask most about frequency, periods, waves and photons — in plain language.

What is frequency, and what is a hertz?+

Frequency measures how often a repeating event happens per unit of time. Its SI unit is the hertz (Hz): 1 Hz means one cycle per second, so a 50 Hz current completes 50 full cycles every second. Frequency is used for sound, light, electricity, rotation, radio waves and any other oscillation.

How do I calculate frequency from period?+

Take the reciprocal: f = 1 ÷ T, with the period T in seconds. For example, a cycle lasting 20 ms = 0.02 s has a frequency of 1 ÷ 0.02 = 50 Hz. The Period tab converts any time unit — nanoseconds to days — and shows the full working.

How do I calculate frequency from wavelength and wave speed?+

Use f = v ÷ λ: divide the wave's speed by its wavelength, both in SI units. Sound at 343 m/s with a 0.78 m wavelength gives 343 ÷ 0.78 ≈ 440 Hz, while light at 299,792,458 m/s with a 650 nm wavelength gives about 461 THz. Keep speed in m/s and wavelength in meters to get hertz directly.

How are frequency and angular frequency related?+

Angular frequency ω counts radians per second instead of cycles per second, and one full cycle is 2π radians, so ω = 2πf and f = ω ÷ 2π. A 60 Hz power grid therefore has ω ≈ 377 rad/s.

How do I convert RPM to hertz?+

RPM is revolutions per minute and hertz is cycles per second, so divide by 60: 3,000 rpm = 50 Hz. To go the other way, multiply hertz by 60 — every result panel here shows the rotation rate in rpm automatically.

How do I find the frequency of light from its energy?+

A photon's energy and frequency are linked by Planck's constant: E = hf, so f = E ÷ h with h = 6.62607015×10−34 J·s. Convert the energy to joules first (1 eV = 1.602176634×10−19 J): a 2.33 eV green photon has a frequency of about 563 THz. The Photon energy tab does all conversions and also outputs the wavelength.

Does frequency change when a wave enters a different medium?+

No. The frequency of a wave is fixed by its source. When light passes from air into glass it slows down, and its wavelength shrinks to λ = v/f — but the frequency, and therefore the color, stays the same.
