04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

How does the text diff checker work?+

Paste the original text in the left box and the modified text in the right box. The tool compares them line by line and highlights lines that were added, removed, or stayed the same.

Can I ignore case or whitespace differences?+

Yes. Open the Advanced options panel and toggle "Ignore case" or "Ignore whitespace" to focus on meaningful content changes instead of formatting noise.

Is there a limit to how much text I can compare?+

No hard limit — the tool runs entirely in your browser, so very large documents may take a moment longer to process on older devices.

Is my text stored or sent anywhere?+

No. Everything is compared locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded, logged, or stored by StudyWithSTEM.

Does it work for code?+

Yes. The diff checker works for any plain text, including source code. It compares line by line, which is exactly how most code diff tools work.
