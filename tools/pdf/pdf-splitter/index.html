04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

How do I split a PDF with this tool?+

Add a PDF file, choose a split mode (extract ranges, split every N pages, or split all pages), then tap Split PDF. Your new files are built on your device and ready to download — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Are my files uploaded to a server?+

No. The split happens entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF never leaves your device, which also means it keeps working without an internet connection once the page has loaded.

Can I extract just a few pages from a PDF?+

Yes. Choose the Extract ranges mode and enter ranges like 1-3,5 to create a single PDF containing only those pages. You can also create multiple separate files by separating ranges with a semicolon, e.g. 1-3; 5-7.

Can I split a PDF into one file per page?+

Yes. Choose the Split all pages mode and the tool will create a separate PDF for every page in the document. You can download them individually or as a ZIP.

Can I rotate pages before splitting?+

Yes. Advanced options includes a rotate button that turns every page 90 degrees at a time — handy for pages that were scanned sideways or upside down.

Is there a limit on file size?+

There's no fixed cap enforced by the tool, but since everything runs in your browser's memory, very large PDFs will depend on your device's available memory.

Will splitting affect the quality of my PDF?+

No. Pages are copied directly from the original file, so text, images, and formatting stay exactly as they were — nothing is re-rendered or compressed.

Is this PDF splitter free to use?+

Yes, it's completely free with no account, no sign-up, and no watermark added to your split files.
