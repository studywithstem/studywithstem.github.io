04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

How do I check if a link is safe with this tool?+

Paste the URL into the input field and tap Check Safety. The tool runs a series of local checks and shows you a safety score with detailed findings for each test.

Is the URL I check sent to a server?+

No. Every check runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. The URL you enter is never uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything.

What does the safety score mean?+

The safety score is a 0–100 rating based on how many risk factors the URL triggers. A higher score means fewer red flags. The detailed results below the score show exactly which checks passed or failed.

What is typosquatting and how does this tool detect it?+

Typosquatting is when attackers register domains that look like popular ones (e.g., gooogle.com instead of google.com). The tool compares the domain against a list of common brands and flags lookalikes using character substitution and similarity checks.

Can this tool detect zero-day phishing sites?+

No tool can catch every brand-new phishing site. This checker uses heuristics — patterns like suspicious keywords, IP-based URLs, and typosquatting — to flag likely threats. Always use your judgment and avoid entering credentials on unfamiliar sites.

Does this tool store the URLs I check?+

No. Nothing is saved, logged, or synced anywhere. Once you leave or refresh the page, every URL you checked is gone for good.

Is this URL safety checker free to use?+

Yes, it's completely free with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many URLs you check.
