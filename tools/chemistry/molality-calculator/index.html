03 · THE FORMULA

One formula, three ways to use it.

Every mode above is the same equation, just solved for a different letter.

Molality

m = n ÷ kg

Moles of solute (n) divided by the mass of solvent in kilograms. If you only have a mass, first find n = mass ÷ molar mass.

Solving for moles

n = m × kg

Multiply the known molality by the mass of solvent in kilograms.

Solving for solvent mass

kg = n ÷ m

Divide the moles of solute by the known molality.

04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

What is molality?+

Molality is a measure of concentration equal to the moles of solute dissolved per kilogram of solvent, written mol/kg and sometimes shown with the symbol m. Unlike molarity, it doesn't change with temperature, since it's based on mass rather than volume.

What is the formula for molality?+

Molality is m = n ÷ kg, where n is the moles of solute and kg is the solvent's mass in kilograms. If you only know the solute's mass, first find moles with n = mass ÷ molar mass, then divide by the solvent's mass.

What's the difference between molality and molarity?+

Molality divides moles of solute by the mass of the solvent in kilograms, while molarity divides moles of solute by the volume of the whole solution in litres. Because molality is mass-based, it stays constant as temperature changes, while molarity can shift slightly as the solution expands or contracts.

How do I find the moles of solute from its mass?+

Divide the solute's mass in grams by its molar mass in grams per mole: n = mass ÷ molar mass. Enter the mass, its unit and the molar mass in the Molality tab and the calculator handles the conversion automatically.

How do I find the mass of solvent for a target molality?+

Rearrange the formula: kg = n ÷ m. Enter the known molality and the moles (or mass plus molar mass) of solute in the Solve solvent mass tab and the calculator returns the required solvent mass in kilograms, grams, pounds and ounces.

What units is molality measured in?+

The standard unit is moles per kilogram (mol/kg). Related units include millimoles per kilogram, micromoles per kilogram, moles per gram and millimoles per gram. The Unit converter tab switches between all of them instantly.

Is this tool free, and where does my data go?+

It's completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded, logged, or sent to any server. No account, no sign-up, no limits.
