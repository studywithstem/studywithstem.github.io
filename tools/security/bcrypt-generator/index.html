04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

What is bcrypt and why should I use it?+

Bcrypt is a password-hashing function designed to be slow and computationally expensive, which makes brute-force attacks much harder. It automatically generates and stores a salt with each hash, so identical passwords produce different hashes.

What does the cost factor (rounds) mean?+

The cost factor determines how many times the hashing algorithm loops — specifically 2cost iterations. Higher cost means slower hashing and better resistance to brute-force attacks. A cost of 10–12 is a common balance for web applications.

Are my passwords sent to a server?+

No. All hashing and verification happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything.

Can I verify a password against a bcrypt hash I already have?+

Yes. Switch to the Verify tab, paste the bcrypt hash and the plaintext password, and the tool will tell you if they match. This runs entirely client-side.

What is the difference between bcrypt and SHA-256?+

SHA-256 is a fast cryptographic hash — designed for speed and integrity, not password storage. Bcrypt is intentionally slow and includes a salt, making it far better for storing passwords. This tool lets you compare both side by side.

How long should a bcrypt hash take?+

A good target is 100–500 milliseconds per hash on your target hardware. Too fast and brute-force becomes easier; too slow and your login system suffers. Adjust the cost factor until you hit that range.

Does this tool store or remember any hash I generate?+

No. Nothing is saved, logged, or synced anywhere. Once you leave or refresh the page, every hash is gone for good.

Is this bcrypt generator free to use?+

Yes, it's completely free with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many hashes you generate.
