04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

What is a time zone converter?+

A time zone converter is a tool that translates a date and time from one time zone to another. It accounts for UTC offsets and daylight saving time so you can see exactly what time it is in another city when it's a specific time in yours.

How do I use this time zone converter?+

Enter a date and time, choose the source time zone, and add the cities or time zones you want to compare. The tool instantly shows the converted time in every selected zone, along with a day/night indicator.

Is my data uploaded to a server?+

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser using JavaScript and your device's built-in time zone database. Nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

Does it handle daylight saving time?+

Yes. The converter uses the IANA time zone database through your browser's Intl API, so it automatically applies daylight saving time rules for the selected date.

How many time zones can I compare at once?+

You can add as many cities or time zones as you like. Each one appears in its own card with the converted time, UTC offset, and a day/night indicator.

Can I plan a meeting across multiple time zones?+

Yes. Enter the meeting time in your own zone and add all the attendees' cities. The tool shows the local time for each city so you can find a slot that works for everyone.

Is this time zone converter free?+

Yes, it's completely free with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many conversions you run.
