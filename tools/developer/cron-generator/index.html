04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

What is a cron expression?+

A cron expression is a string of five or six fields separated by spaces that tells a scheduler when to run a job. The fields represent minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week, and optionally year, using wildcards, ranges, lists and step values.

How do I use this cron generator?+

Pick a preset or enter your own cron expression. The tool validates it, explains it in plain English, and shows the next few run times. Copy the expression into your crontab or scheduler.

Is my cron expression uploaded to a server?+

No. Parsing, validating and next-run calculation all happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device.

What do the five fields in a cron expression mean?+

In order: minute (0–59), hour (0–23), day of month (1–31), month (1–12 or JAN–DEC), and day of week (0–6 or SUN–SAT). Some systems add a sixth field for seconds or a seventh for year.

Can I schedule a job to run every 5 minutes?+

Yes. Use */5 * * * * — the */5 means every 5th minute, and the remaining asterisks mean every hour, every day, every month, every day of the week.

Does this tool support seconds and years?+

Yes. Simply type a 6-field or 7-field expression into the main input and the tool will parse and explain it. The visual editor shows the standard 5-field layout, but the parser handles extended formats.

How do I read a cron expression I found in a config file?+

Paste it into the cron expression box. The tool parses it, shows the schedule in plain English, and lists the next five run times.

Is this cron generator free to use?+

Yes, it's completely free with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many times you use it.
