04 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short answers — with the long ones one tap away.

What is a calendar generator?+

A calendar generator is a tool that lets you create, view and export calendars for any month or year. You can add events, switch between monthly, weekly and yearly views, and download the calendar as an ICS or CSV file.

How do I use this calendar generator?+

Pick a month and year, choose a view (monthly, weekly, yearly), and optionally add events. Then export the calendar as ICS or CSV. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Is my calendar data uploaded to a server?+

No. All calendar generation, event handling and export happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device.

Can I add events to the calendar?+

Yes. Click any day to add an event with a title, time and optional description. Events are stored in your browser and can be exported as ICS or CSV.

What export formats are supported?+

You can export your calendar as an ICS file (importable into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook) or as a CSV file for spreadsheets.

Can I import events from a file?+

Yes. You can import events from a CSV file with columns for date, title, time and description.

Does this tool work offline?+

Yes, once the page is loaded, all processing happens in your browser. You can use it without an internet connection.

Is this calendar generator free to use?+

Yes, it's completely free with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many calendars you create.
